I am a PhD student in Governance at the Centre for Sustainability of the Hertie School, supervised by [Lion Hirth](https://www.hertie-school.org/en/who-we-are/profile/person/hirth). 

My research uses empirical and causal methods to learn how electricity markets actually behave from observed market and operational data, and uses that to inform policy. I currently work on three areas: **market power**, **demand response** and **subsidy design**. A common thread is producing credible counterfactuals for policy-relevant questions in energy markets.

Before my PhD I completed a Master in Public Policy at the Hertie School, where my thesis studied the effects of the Iberian cap on final prices for consumers and gas use during the energy crisis. I hold a Bachelor in Philosophy, Politics and Economics from a joint programme of UC3M, UPF and UAM.

Currently visiting [Mar Reguant](https://mreguant.github.io/) at the Barcelona School of Economics.
